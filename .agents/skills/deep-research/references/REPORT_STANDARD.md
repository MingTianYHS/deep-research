# Report standard

Reports contain:

1. executive conclusion;
2. scope, date window, and assumptions;
3. supported findings;
4. conflicting or weakening evidence;
5. new information this run;
6. risks and uncertainty;
7. unresolved questions and next actions;
8. source list.

Citations appear near claims and must resolve to accepted evidence cards. Distinguish event, publication, and access dates. State unavailable sources, failed tools, budget stops, and single-source areas; never imply completeness.
